## What this snapshot changes

**1. Scope is a closed list of 37, not a judgment call.** 13 + 24. Discern covers the Senate list
and says so. No school gets added because it feels relevant.

**2. Notable absences — and this is exactly what a discerner needs told plainly.** Schools UMC
candidates commonly assume are fine that are **not** on the Senate list as of this capture:
**Asbury Theological Seminary**, **Truett Theological Seminary (Baylor)**, **Seminary of the
Southwest**. That does not make them bad schools or bar a candidate — but it means the graduate
requirement of ¶324.4 is not met by the degree itself, which is a very different and much harder
conversation with a Board of Ordained Ministry. Verify each against the current list before
saying so on the site, and state it as a fact about the Senate list, never as a judgment about
the school.

**3. The online rule is the single most decision-changing fact on the page.** Every UMC school
can deliver a fully online MDiv that counts. **No** non-UMC Senate school can have *any* online or
distance class counted toward the requirement. A candidate at Princeton or Fuller who takes one
class online has a credit that does not count for ordination. That is not a footnote — it is a
first-class, prominently-rendered field on every tier-2 page.

**4. Two fields in `content/seminaries.ts` are already stale.**
- `Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary` → GBHEM now lists **Garrett Seminary**.
- Claremont's location — the page gives no city, so this remains open; verify independently.
Also: our `modalities` array reads as a description of what a school *offers*, but for the
tier-2 schools the ordination-relevant fact is what *counts*. Those are different things and the
model must not conflate them.
