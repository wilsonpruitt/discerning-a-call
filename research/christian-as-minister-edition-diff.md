## Headline finding: the **1996 General Conference** is the hinge

The dataset splits cleanly pre/post-1996:

- **1977 / 1985 / 1988** — built around the *"diaconal minister"* (a **consecrated lay** office) and
  "associate member." The **deacon is only a transitional step** toward elder. No "provisional
  member," no permanent "Order of Deacon."
- **2009 / 2013** — *"provisional member"* and a **permanent Order of Deacon** parallel to elder;
  **"commissioning"** is the new act that *begins* provisional membership; "diaconal" survives only
  as a legacy / transition note.

The candidacy **ladder** itself reshaped:

| 1988 (3rd) | 2009 (7th) |
|---|---|
| Step 1 Inquiring → Step 2 Exploring → Step 3 Declared → Certified | Certified (Step 4) → **annual Candidacy Renewal** (Step 6) → **Completion toward Provisional Membership** (Step 7), now **split into parallel deacon / elder tracks** |

## Why this matters for *Discerning a Call*

The site's `/process` ladder is grounded in the **2024 BoD** (current law). This edition history is
the *backstory* of that ladder — useful for: (1) explaining to callers why older mentors describe the
path differently (pre-1996 vocabulary still circulates); (2) a possible "how the path has changed"
explainer; (3) sourcing the discernment-mentor training module (below) with the historical arc.
