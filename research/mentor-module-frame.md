# Discernment-mentor module — framing doc

Working frame for a future **Discerning a Call** section that *trains and resources discernment
mentors* (candidacy mentors / supervising pastors / those who walk with someone testing a call).
Decided 2026-06-17 from the *Christian as Minister* edition research; see
`candidacy-ladder-sidebyside.md` and `christian-as-minister-edition-diff.md` for the source.

## The core finding that sets the frame

We checked whether the **2013 (8th) edition's** treatment of mentorship could be the frame.
**It can't — it's too thin.** Every 2013 mention treats the mentor as an *administrative
assignment*, never a described practice:

- *"Apply to the district superintendent… to be admitted to the candidacy process **and assigned
  a candidacy mentor**."*
- *(deaconess/home-missioner track)* *"is **matched with a mentor/coach**…"*
- Front matter names a *"**Group Candidacy Mentoring** setting"* and the conference
  *"**vocational discernment coordinator**."*
- Author's title: Meg Lassiat, *"Director of Candidacy, **Mentoring**, and Conference Relations."*

It tells you a mentor *exists* and *when one is assigned* — never what a mentor **does**, what makes
a good one, or what the relationship is *for*. The structural reason: **_The Christian as Minister_
is the candidate's book, not the mentor's.** The mentor's counterpart resources are GBHEM's separate
*Candidacy Mentoring* / Mentoring Workbook and the *Candidacy Guidebook*.

**The richer functional frame is actually in the _older_ (1985 / 1988) editions** — same booklet,
lost in later revision:

> *"The supervising pastor serves as 'mentor' or 'facilitator' to the candidate as s/he examines the
> call in light of biblical records, the role and function of United Methodist clergy, his/her
> personal gifts and graces, and evidences of leadership style. Utilizing new or confirmed
> insights… the candidate then makes a commitment to the form of ministry felt to be most
> appropriate."*

That is a real frame: **the mentor as a discernment companion who helps the candidate test the call
against four things — Scripture, the actual shape of clergy life, the candidate's own gifts, and
their leadership.** Relational and formational, not procedural.

## The frame: a synthesis (don't anchor on any single edition)

| Source | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| **1985 / 1988 editions** | The *function* — mentor as **facilitator** who helps test the call. The relational core. |
| **2013 edition** | The current *vocabulary and structures* — "candidacy mentor," **group** mentoring, the conference vocational-discernment-coordinator context. |
| **BOM Handbook, Ch. 7** (authoritative) | The official *duties, criteria, training, accountability* — see the section below. **This is now pulled** (was the open TODO). |

**One line:** *mentor as discernment companion (older frame) + current terminology and group/conference
structures (2013) + the authoritative GBHEM role definition (BOM Handbook Ch. 7).*

## What the BOM Handbook (Chapter 7 — Candidacy Mentoring) actually says

Pulled 2026-06-17 from the GBHEM *Board of Ordained Ministry Handbook* (the page is HTML, not a real
PDF; raw HTML scraped + de-tagged). **This revises our earlier read:** GBHEM defines the
candidacy-mentor role *thoroughly* — far more than the 2013 booklet implied. The booklet was thin
because it is the candidate's book; the Handbook is where the mentor role lives.

**The governing definition (¶349.1, quoted in the Handbook):**
> *"Mentoring occurs within a relationship where the mentor takes responsibility for creating a safe
> place for reflection and growth… Mentoring is distinct from the evaluative and supervisory process
> that is a part of preparation for ministry."*

**Two categories of mentor (¶349.2):** **Candidacy Mentor** (for inquiring/declared/certified
candidates) and **Clergy Mentor** (for local pastors + provisional members — that one is Connexion's
lane, see Ch. 17). Candidacy Mentors are *"clergy in full connection, associate members or local
pastors who have completed the Course of Study."*

**The candidacy mentor's role (Handbook's own words):** *"co-discerner, consultant and catalyst in a
way that provides freedom, confidentiality, flexibility and stability for the candidate in discerning
God's call."* Encourages/nurtures across "vocational and life decisions"; works through
*Answering the Call: Candidacy Guidebook*; records progress in the online system (PassageUMC).

**Selection & assignment (¶349.2a):** DS suggests clergy → BOM (via Candidacy Registrar + Vocational
Discernment Coordinator) trains & confirms → **DS assigns** a mentor when a candidate is admitted.

**Criteria for who serves:** spiritually mature; strong understanding of call across lay/licensed/
ordained; keeps developing vocationally; can draw others into deep vocational reflection; open/flexible;
respected by colleagues; life congruent with clergy standards. Explicitly: **mentors should be
*diverse* — deacons, elders, AND local pastors; varied age, gender, ethnicity, family lifestyle.**

**Training (every 4 years):** theological grounding for mentoring; **distinguishing
supervision vs. evaluation vs. mentoring**; the meaning of confidentiality (incl. in a group); the
current process + Passage; familiarity with *The Christian as Minister* + the Guidebook.

**Responsibilities:** pray with/for; set a **covenant** + meeting schedule; study the Guidebook
together; keep confidentiality; discuss the candidate's upcoming P/S-PRC & charge-conference meetings;
file annual mentor reports; attend the initial certification interview with the dCOM.

**Vocational Discernment Coordinator (VDC):** the conference role that *recruits, trains, and assigns*
mentors and runs **group candidacy mentoring** "wherever possible" — i.e. group is the GBHEM default,
1:1 the fallback.

### The honest re-read of the "wide lane"

GBHEM defines **who** a mentor is and **what duties** they carry quite fully. What it *doesn't* supply
is the **practical "how" of mentoring well** — the actual discernment conversations, how to test a
call, how to adapt to a youth vs. a second-career mentee, how to run a healthy group. The Handbook
even *says* training should cover "theological grounding for mentoring" but provides none. **That gap
— formation practice, life-stage adaptation, ready-to-use conversation guides — is Discern's lane,**
and it's wide. We complement the Handbook's role definition; we don't restate it.

## The four lenses (the spine of the module)

Adapted from the 1985/88 "facilitator" passage — what a mentor helps a candidate examine:

1. **Scripture** — testing the call against the biblical witness on calling and ministry.
2. **The reality of clergy life** — the actual role and function of UM clergy, not the romance of it.
3. **Gifts and graces** — honest naming of the candidate's gifts, and the gaps.
4. **Leadership** — evidence of how the candidate leads, in real settings.

The mentor's job is not to *gatekeep* or *certify* (that's the dCOM's role) — it's to **accompany the
testing**, then help the candidate *name a fitting form of ministry* (elder / deacon / local pastor /
lay / deaconess–home missioner).

## Design principles (carry over from the rest of the site)

- **Bias-to-Yes, not gatekeeping.** The mentor opens doors and helps a person take the *next step*;
  the formal screening lives with the SPRC / Charge Conference / dCOM. Keep the module's tone
  "here's how to walk with someone," not "here's how to vet them." (Consistent with the site's
  [[seven-levers-lens]] posture.)
- **Honor the vocabulary drift.** Mentors ordained pre-1996 carry older terms (probationary, deacon
  -as-step). The `/process` history section already flags this for *candidates*; the mentor module
  should equip *mentors* to translate between eras — and to notice when their own map is dated.
- **Group + 1:1.** Frame mentoring as *both* one-on-one and the **Group Candidacy Mentoring** model
  (already live in the Rio Texas conference pack as "Group Mentoring"). Don't assume solo.
- **Life-stage aware.** The site's differentiator is meeting callers by life stage; a mentor module
  should help mentors adjust for youth vs. second-career vs. in-seminary mentees.

## What this module is NOT

- Not the candidacy *process* (that's `/process`).
- Not a substitute for GBHEM's official Candidacy Mentoring training or conference certification of
  mentors — it points to those, it doesn't replace them.
- Not a screening/assessment tool.
